JharSetu — SIH26043 §01 · What We're Building
01

What We're Building

Platform for Govt. of Jharkhand, SIH26043 (Disaster Management theme, 24h build, 100 marks). Not a complaint portal. A challenge exchange: villager voice note → verified, funded, staffed, tracked project with evidence.

Landing Headline
"See a need. Form a team. Close the loop."
Every university becomes the R&D department of its district.

The Full Loop

Report → Compile → Verify → Prioritise → Match → Propose → Pilot → Pledge (Resource Swarm) → Deliver with evidence → Impact Verified

Two Operational Modes

Peace Mode (Default)
Long-term challenges: education, health, farming, preparedness. Standard workflow, proposal competition, pilot phases.
Crisis Mode
Triggered by real IMD/CAP alert or mock drill. Red top band + red districts + crisis room (needs, gaps, nearby resources, SMS reports, tech-squad call-up). Fast path: OPEN → DEPLOYED.

Geographic Scope

Region-first for Jharkhand (24 districts), with live region switch to Rajkot (college round). Every seeded number labeled "Simulated scenario".

What This Is — and Is Not

✓ This is ✗ Not this
A challenge exchange: voice → verified → funded → tracked A complaint portal (no feedback loop, no evidence, no closure)
University–industry collaboration platform with NEP 2020 credit An emergency dispatch system (that's 112's job)
Evidence-gated closure (photos + beneficiary count + sign-off) A disaster prediction AI
Hash-chained Impact Ledger for audit + CSR/NAAC PDFs A general chatbot or payments system

Three Pitch Ideas

These are the three demo beats that must land in every presentation:

Pitch Idea 1 — Challenge Compiler
Hindi/English voice/photo/text/SMS → structured brief + confidence score + "AI is unsure about…" + human approval gate. One call in, one brief out, every step timed and shown in the trace panel.
Pitch Idea 2 — Matching + Resource Swarm
List what's already nearby first (PostGIS radius query), then recommend university + company + NGO with reasons scored on 5 factors. Multiple partial pledges from different orgs fill a live gap bar. 8 pledges + 4 pledges = gap closes on stage.
Pitch Idea 3 — Impact Ledger
Closes only with photos + beneficiary count + volunteer check + community sign-off. Every action is hash-chained. Auto-generates CSR/NAAC/NIRF accreditation PDFs. Ledger can be independently verified at GET /api/ledger/verify.

Three Demo Beats (repeat everywhere)

  • Readiness Score 48 vs 84 — cheap deployable wins over impressive-but-impractical
  • Crisis drill + live SMS — airplane mode on phone, SMS arrives with exact pin on map
  • Silent zones — high hazard + real population + almost no reports = digital divide visualised

Roles & Demo Switcher

Six seeded accounts — one per role. One-click role switcher, no passwords on stage. Roles are real Supabase sessions enforced by PostgreSQL RLS, not hidden buttons.

Citizen
Report in <1 min. Track status. Confirm fix. No account required for reporting. Name and number never published.
Field Volunteer / NGO
Field verify with photo + sources. List resources in registry. Confirm work on the ground.
Coordinator
District officer / faculty. Approve briefs. Rate readiness (1–5). Approve pilots. Close with evidence. Start drill. Override AI ranking.
University
Adopt challenge. Build team (skill-gap plan). Submit PDF proposal. Execute pilot stages. Post progress updates.
Industry / Company
Pledge materials (partial allowed). Get auto-generated CSR PDF. Message the college. Track delivery.
Admin
Verify orgs. Manage regions. Edit formula weights (logged). Award competition windows early. Full history of any challenge.

Architecture Decision
NGOs are their own role (migration 0013_ngo_role.sql), separate from industry. Companies give materials; NGOs give money. They see different default boards.

JharSetu — SIH26043 §07 · The Five Formulas
07

The Five Formulas

All formulas are deterministic code. AI proposes inputs (severity, capability similarity); humans override. Admin-editable weights are logged in the ledger.

Formula 1 — Priority Score (0–100)

Priority Score = sum of weighted factors (total capped at 100)
25 ptsSeverity (1–5). AI proposes; coordinator overrides.
15 ptsUrgency (deadline proximity + worsening rate)
Crisis Mode floors urgency at 80%. Inside 72h deadline it starts to dominate.
15 ptsPeople affected (log scale, saturates at 5,000)
15 ptsVulnerability (children, elderly, disability, pregnancy, medical, isolation, no_signal)
Weighted individually (medical_dependency=1.0 → no_signal=0.5). Divisor = 3.4 (worst realistic combo).
10 ptsHazard exposure (0–1 from GIS hazard_cells)
10 ptsResource gap (% of need still unpledged)
5 ptsRecurrence (seasonal/repeated, saturates at 3×)
5 ptsCommunity signal (unique reporters, log scale, CAPPED)
Reporter count saturates fast — capped so loud urban areas cannot outrank remote villages.
= 100Total

Bands: ≥75 Critical 50–74 High 25–49 Moderate <25 Long-term

Formula 2 — Confidence Ladder

Answers "is it real?" (separate from priority). Never hidden. An inaccurate flag drops one rung. AI never rejects.

unverified→ community_corroborated
(≥3 reporters)→ externally_corroborated
(AI found independent proof)→ field_verified→ coordinator_approved→ resolved_with_evidence

Plain SMS from unknown number starts at bottom. External corroboration = AI checked Open-Meteo weather + news + web and cited every source. From externally_corroborated up, problems are open to colleges.

Formula 3 — Deduplication & Clustering

With semantic embeddings (Gemini)
cosine ≥ 0.85AND ≤ 2 km AND ≤ 30 days → merge
0.75–0.85AND same district → review (coordinator decides)
Lexical fallback (no embedding key)
same categoryAND ≤ 1 km AND ≤ 7 days → merge
same categoryAND ≤ 2 km → review

No-GPS report: same district + similarity ≥ 0.90 → merge. A report with no GPS is not automatically a new problem.

Formula 4 — Capability Matching (0–100)

Resource-first: list registry items already in radius BEFORE proposing new builds
40 ptsCapability fit (embedding similarity; fallback: keyword overlap)
20 ptsAvailability (free capacity now)
15 ptsProximity (decay half-life 35 km; unknown = 0.3)
15 ptsResource fit (% of open gap this partner could cover)
10 ptsPartner type: univ (track record + interest) | company (CSR + district) | NGO (local presence + speed)

Every recommendation carries its reasons. Coordinator can override the ranking (override written to ledger).

Formula 5 — Solution Readiness Score (0–100)

Each factor rated 1–5 by coordinator or faculty mentor. Cost/time/resources pre-filled from proposal + registry.
20 ptsTechnical feasibility
15 ptsCost feasibility (cost ≤ ₹50k → 5; ≤₹2L → 4; ≤₹7.5L → 3; ≤₹25L → 2; else 1)
15 ptsTime to deploy (≤30d → 5; ≤90d → 4; ≤180d → 3; ≤365d → 2; else 1)
15 ptsLocal resource availability (from registry coverage %)
15 ptsSafety
10 ptsCommunity acceptance (from community validation signals)
10 ptsScalability
The Demo Beat That Lands Hardest
Sensor-AI prediction system: 48 / 100 (impressive but impractical).
Low-cost siren relay + shelter mapping: 84 / 100 (cheap + deployable next month). The 84 wins the pilot.

Lifecycle + State Machine

Main Path

REPORTED→ REFINED
AI brief drafted→ VERIFIED
coordinator approved→ OPEN→ TEAM_FORMED→ SOLUTION_PROPOSED→ PILOT→ DEPLOYED→ IMPACT_VERIFIED

Crisis Fast Path (crisisOnly)

OPEN→ DEPLOYED (crisis) — evidence still required

Side Paths (human-only, reason required)

DUPLICATE NEEDS_FOLLOW_UP CLOSED_NOT_ACTIONABLE — reporter is told why. AI never rejects.

Transition Rules (from lifecycle.ts)

FromToWhoRequirements
REPORTEDREFINEDcoordinator, adminbrief drafted
REFINEDVERIFIEDcoordinator, adminbrief drafted
VERIFIEDOPENcoordinator, admin—
OPENTEAM_FORMEDpartner rolesteam formed
TEAM_FORMEDSOLUTION_PROPOSEDpartner rolesproposal submitted
SOLUTION_PROPOSEDPILOTcoordinator, adminpilot approved
PILOTDEPLOYEDcoordinator, adminevidence + beneficiary count + verifier sign-off
DEPLOYEDIMPACT_VERIFIEDcitizen, volunteer, coordinator, admincommunity confirmation
OPEN / TEAM_FORMEDDEPLOYEDcoordinator (crisis only)evidence + count + sign-off

Evidence Requirements for Closure

  • At least one photo or document as evidence
  • Beneficiary count (number of people actually served)
  • Field volunteer or coordinator verifier sign-off
  • Community confirmation (IMPACT_VERIFIED stage)
Hard Rule
POST /api/challenges/:id/deploy returns HTTP 422 with every unmet requirement listed in error.message when any of the above is missing. The UI must render this verbatim.

Proposal Competition Window (Decision §9)

First proposal on a verified problem opens a 2–14 day window (by severity). Highest-scoring viable proposal wins when it closes. If none is viable, the window reopens for revision. Admins/coordinators can award early: POST /api/admin/windows/:id/award.

Auto-release

Claims are automatically released after 14 days of no progress. Reputation system tracks abandonment.

Connectivity Ladder + SMS

1
Internet available
Normal upload — text + GPS location first, then audio/photo. Full trace panel. Realtime Supabase subscriptions.
2
No internet + signal + Critical (sev 4–5 or vulnerable groups)
Coded SMS via sms: link — one tap, pre-filled. Format: JS1 K7F2 W5 25.2481,87.6412 P200 VCE "pani nahi 2 din"
3
Basic phone (feature phone)
Plain Hindi SMS to the JharSetu number. Server parses and files. Confidence starts at bottom of ladder. Reply confirms receipt.
4
No signal (offline)
IndexedDB outbox queue + service worker retry. Idempotent client_id ensures no duplicate once connectivity restores. Volunteer assists on field if available.

Coded SMS Format (≤160 characters)

JS1 K7F2 W5 25.2481,87.6412 P200 VCE "pani nahi 2 din"
 │   │    │  └─────────────── GPS coordinates
 │   │    └─── category + severity (W=water, 5=critical)
 │   └──────── report ID
 └──────────── version 1

P200    = 200 people affected
VCE     = vulnerable: Children, Elderly [C/E/D/P/M/I/N]
"…"     = brief description in local language
SMS Reply
"JharSetu: report K7F2 received. For life-threatening emergencies, call 112."

Notes

  • GPS only receives (incl. NavIC) — cannot transmit from browser; used to embed coordinates in SMS before sending
  • Tower location + auto-SMS = native-app roadmap (browser cannot do this)
  • Coded SMS lands with an exact pin on the crisis room map during the demo drill
  • Production = licensed provider + TRAI DLT registration. Demo = spare Android + SMS Gateway for Android app
  • Full sync later merges via the same report ID (idempotent)

Roadmap (Not Built — Say So)

  • Missed call / IVR / USSD
  • WhatsApp / Telegram (needs internet)
  • Bhashini / Sarvam for Santali / Mundari / Ho (tribal languages)

17

Key Architectural Decisions (docs/DECISIONS.md)

Decision 1 — Backend is Source of Truth

The backend/ app holds the intake pipeline, Challenge Compiler, dedup, priority, matching, readiness, lifecycle, ledger, RLS, and migrations. Frontend's app/api/* routes were a demo stand-in from hour 1 and are superseded. backend/lib/seed/* and backend/scripts/seed-demo.ts are canonical seed.

Decision 2 — One Origin, Not Two (Same-Origin Cookie)

frontend/next.config.ts rewrites /api/* to BACKEND_ORIGIN. The browser only ever talks to the frontend's own origin. Eliminates cross-origin cookie issues (Safari third-party cookie blocking, Chrome phase-out). CORS removed entirely for browser calls.

BACKEND_ORIGIN unset → demo routes answer (seeded data)
BACKEND_ORIGIN set   → real backend answers (no page changes)

Decision 3 — Reads via Service-Role Client

All reads go through supabaseAdmin ?? supabase (service-role). The SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY has no NEXT_PUBLIC_ prefix and is only imported in app/api/* — never sent to browser. Anonymous GET on challenges would return empty (RLS blocks anon selects).

Decision 4 — Matches Table as Write-Through Cache

The matches table is a write-through cache. First call (or ?refresh=true) invokes computeMatches and upserts results. Subsequent calls return instantly. Seed does NOT pre-populate: matches are computed against live database state, not seed time state.

Decision 5 — NGO as Separate Role

Migration 0013_ngo_role.sql. Companies give materials; NGOs give money. Different default boards, different RLS policies (0014_ngo_rls_parity.sql).

Decision 6 — Proposal Competition Window

First proposal on a verified problem opens a 2–14 day window (by severity). Highest-scoring viable proposal wins. Reviewer marks not-viable with required changes. Window reopens if no viable proposal at close. Early award: POST /api/admin/windows/:id/award. Rule-based scoring if no AI key.

Decision 7 — Dedup Without Semantic Embeddings

With no embedding key, local hashing vectoriser scores differently-worded reports of the same flood around 0.4 (below 0.85/0.75 thresholds). Fallback: same category within 1 km in 7 days = merge. Within 2 km = review. Semantic embeddings keep the original bars.

Decision 8 — Progress Updates Belong to a Stage

progress_updates.stage_id is NOT NULL. General update with no stage → filed against the in-progress stage, else next undone stage, else last stage.

Decision 9 — End-to-End Test Script

backend/scripts/e2e-flow.ts: report → AI check → verifier → PDF proposal → award → requirements → 5kg + 5kg + money pledges → sent/received → messages → stage + update → admin record, metrics, assistant → public tracking, with role-wall checks. Rows titled [E2E TEST]. --cleanup deletes them. Ledger rows stay (append-only).

Decision 10 — We Train Our Own Models (reverses earlier "APIs only" stance)

An earlier objections-handling answer said "use APIs and embeddings, don't train our own ML models." That is superseded. Faculty evaluation requires original trained work, so we now train our own models on top of — not instead of — the existing rule-based/API fallback chain (AI_ENABLED=false must still work).

Approved targets, in order: (1) report classifier — category + hazard + severity, fine-tuned MuRIL/IndicBERT or a TF-IDF+logreg baseline, trained on the 40 hand-labeled reports + seed data + public disaster-tweet corpora (HumAID/CrisisNLP); augments backend/lib/ai/fallback.ts. (2) dedup embeddings — contrastive-fine-tuned multilingual MiniLM, augmenting the hashing vectoriser in backend/lib/ai/local-embed.ts. Report accuracy/precision/recall against the rule-based baseline and the LLM on the same held-out set. Stretch: vulnerability-tag extraction, silent-zone report-expectation model, evidence-photo plausibility check.

Unchanged: still no disaster prediction, still no general chatbot, still AI-assisted-recommendation-only (human approves), still must degrade cleanly with AI keys removed.
