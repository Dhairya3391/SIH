The loop

Six steps from a spoken sentence to a proven fix.

Most platforms stop at collecting reports. The hard part is everything after.

01 Report

Speak in Hindi, Santali or Kurukh. No form, no account, no English.

02 Compile

Thirty similar reports become one clear brief, not thirty tickets.

03 Verify

A volunteer confirms it on the ground before anyone spends money.

04 Match

A university lab, a CSR budget and a district officer, with reasons shown.

05 Build

Partners pledge equipment and people against a live, visible gap.

06 Prove

Nothing closes without photographs and a community confirmation.

Equity by design

The places that report nothing are not the places with no problems.

A platform that only ranks what gets reported rewards whoever shouts loudest. JharSetu compares hazard exposure and population against how many reports actually arrive, and flags the gap.

A district with high mining risk, sixty thousand residents and two reports is not quiet. It is unheard, and it belongs at the top of a survey list rather than the bottom of a queue.

Tamper-evident record

Nothing closes without evidence.

Closing a challenge needs photographs from before and after, a count of people served, a named verifier, and a confirmation from the community that reported it. Every action is written to an append-only ledger where each entry is hashed against the one before it.

Edit any past row and the chain stops matching. It is not a blockchain and does not pretend to be one — it simply makes a quiet edit impossible to hide.

Who it is for

Six consoles, one shared record.

Citizen

Report a need by voice or SMS, track what happened to it, and confirm when it is genuinely fixed.

Field volunteer

Visit the site, photograph what is there, and raise the confidence of a report that deserves it.

District coordinator

Work a ranked queue that explains itself, approve briefs, and score proposals on seven readiness factors.

University

Find real problems that fit your lab, form a skill-gap team, and propose something that can actually deploy.

Company or NGO

See exactly what is still unfunded, pledge against a specific gap, and get evidence your CSR report can use.

State administrator

Tune the scoring weights, audit every override, and verify the ledger has not been touched.
