- **9:20–9:30** — Say target out loud, hand out role cards, start timer.
  Announce **9:50, 10:10, 10:25** to the room.
- **9:30–10:00** — Five slides:
  1. Title + one-liner
  2. Somra + three groups who never meet (citizens / universities / industry)
  3. Jharkhand numbers (2,400+ lightning deaths in a decade, state relief portal pilot Dhanbad/Bokaro)
  4. 7 official components × "live now / by H12 / by H18"

- **10:00–10:10** — 3-min script with each person's single line (see tasks/README).
  Check every claim is **true right now**.
- **10:10–10:25** — Two timed rehearsals. Screenshots as backup.
